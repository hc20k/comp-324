What is domain certificate and why do we need it? Please research it online and explain in simple English.
	A domain certificate verifies the ownership of a specific website. Basically it makes sure that the website you're visiting isn't being hosted by an attacker that wants to steal information, so it's secure to enter secure information into.

 What is X509? Please research it online and explain in simple English.
	An X509 certificate is a digital document that verifies identities and communications over the internet. It prevents "man in the middle" attacks, so nobody else can read your sensitive data except for the intended recipient.

Why do we need to set expiration dates for domain certificates? Please research it online and explain in simple English.
	It's good to have an expiration date for a domain certificate just in case the encryption standard becomes obsolete, the ownership of the domain changes, or if the cert/private key gets leaked.

You have created a self-signed domain certificate in this lab. What is the difference between a Domain certificate and Self-Signed Cert? Please research it online and explain in simple English.
	For public websites, a domain certificate is good to ensure users can trust and safely use your site. For internal testing purposes, a self-signed cert is better because trust isn't much of a concern. Self-signed certs are also free, and domain certs cost money.
